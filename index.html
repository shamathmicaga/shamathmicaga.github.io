About Me

A creative mind in a
tech-first world.

I'm Shamathmica G A, a first-year B.Tech CSIT student at Reva University, Bengaluru. I live at the crossroads of art and technology — whether I'm sketching fashion silhouettes, building interfaces, editing a reel, or writing a fictional world into existence.

Beyond academics, I'm actively shaping campus culture through three communities: designing for Narcissa, storytelling through Under 25, and giving back via the Reva Student Council. I bring the same energy to events and marketing as I do to a blank canvas.
